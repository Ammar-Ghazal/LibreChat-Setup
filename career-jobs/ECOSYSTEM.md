Alpha timing note: its page displayed an October 11 deadline and September 2026
cohort-2 application language when checked. It also had conflicting one-year and
three-year graduation windows for its Founder Track. Do not resolve that conflict
by guessing; verify the current application or ask the organizer. Dates for the
January/March program were displayed without a consistently explicit year.

## Eligibility handling

Read citizenship/work-status facts from the private profile. Work authorization,
employer sponsorship, export-control access and security clearance are distinct.
Do not automatically include or exclude every SpaceX role based on citizenship.
[This SpaceX posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8560537002)
states U.S. status categories or eligibility for required government authorizations.
For any recommendation, verify the specific current posting and flag unresolved
requirements for employer/legal confirmation; do not promise eligibility.

## Fields for a future searchable ecosystem catalog

- Organization, canonical domain and type: employer, VC, talent network,
  engineering community, fellowship or founder accelerator.
- Relationships: portfolio company and backing firm, with source and date;
  current/historical/uncertain investment status when evidence allows.
- Relevant specialties, geography, stage and employment pathways.
- Funding announcement date, round, amount, currency, investors and source;
  unknowns stay unknown. A funding amount is not runway or proof of stability.
- Official careers URL and supported collector identity, if verified.
- Program track, application URL, eligibility, deadline/timezone, attendance,
  cost/stipend, investment/equity terms where applicable and evidence checked date.
- Fit rationale tied to the profile, required preparation and uncertainties.

Future collections should preserve provenance and refresh these facts. Keep this
catalog separate from sources.json: the running jobs collector currently accepts
only supported Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby board configurations.
